# Broken Knight Brand Assets

The identity centers on a crowned close-helm split by a glowing fracture: a visual shorthand for a fallen or damaged knight who still carries royal authority.

## Files

- `broken_knight_logo_v1.png` — transparent full game logo and wordmark.
- `broken_knight_icon_master_v1.png` — transparent 1024 px square application-icon master.
- `broken_knight_icon_256_v1.png` — lightweight 256 px preview/icon.
- `broken_knight.ico` — Windows icon containing 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, and 256 px layers.
- `broken_knight_splash_v1.png` — loading-screen version of the full logo.

The full logo is used for the Godot loading splash and pause screen. The crest icon is used by the window, desktop launcher, HUD, hero inventory, and admin interface.

## Palette

- Deep cobalt blue
- Near-black forged steel
- Antique royal gold
- Restrained crimson plume
